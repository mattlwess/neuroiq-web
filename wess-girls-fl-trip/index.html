From the magic of the Disney castle to the roar of Universal's coasters to the calm blue Gulf at Longboat Key — here's the trip, told in our favorite photos and a few fun facts from each place we went.

Walt Disney World

📍 Lake Buena Vista, Orlando

Where the whole trip felt like a storybook. We wished on the castle, chased characters, and stayed for the fireworks. The most magical day of the week.

🏰

The Castle

Cinderella Castle stands 189 ft tall and has 27 towers — but not a single elevator inside!

🎆

Fireworks Nightly

The evening castle show lights up the sky with projections, lasers and hundreds of shells.

🌍

Bigger Than a City

Disney World spans about 25,000 acres — roughly the size of San Francisco.

🍦

Dole Whip & Treats

The famous pineapple Dole Whip is a park tradition — a must every single visit.

Fun fact: cast members are trained to always point with two fingers — never just one!

Universal Orlando

📍 Universal Blvd, Orlando

Adrenaline day! Wizards, coasters, and movie worlds you can walk right into. We screamed on every ride — twice.

⚡

Wizarding World

Sip Butterbeer and wander Diagon Alley & Hogsmeade — connected by the Hogwarts Express.

🎢

Coaster Country

Some Universal coasters hit 65+ mph — hold on tight and keep your hands up!

🎬

Movies, Real

Ride-throughs put you inside your favorite films with 3D and giant sets.

🧈

Butterbeer

Frozen, cold, or hot — millions of cups are served every year. Frozen wins.

Fun fact: wave your interactive wand at the right shop window and you can cast "spells" all over the park!

The Resort at Longboat Key Club

📍 Longboat Key, Gulf Coast

After the theme-park thrills, pure calm: powder-soft sand, palm shade, and Gulf sunsets. The perfect way to end the trip.

🏖️

Powdery Beaches

The Gulf shore here is famous for soft, quartz-white sand and warm, gentle water.

🌅

Sunset Side

Longboat Key faces west — front-row seats to the Gulf of Mexico sunset every evening.

⛳

Resort Life

The club is known for golf, tennis, and a marina tucked along a barrier island.

🐬

Dolphin Waters

Bottlenose dolphins are a common sight playing just offshore in Sarasota Bay.

Fun fact: Longboat Key is a barrier island about 10 miles long — but only a few blocks wide!

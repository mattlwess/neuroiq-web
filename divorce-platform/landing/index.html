Why this is hard

Splitting a life's assets is where trust breaks down.

When a marriage ends, dividing what you own is slow, opaque, and adversarial — and neither side can see whether the money actually moved the way the agreement said it would.

The blind spot

Nobody sees the same numbers

Assets get valued in spreadsheets, sold through separate brokers, and settled by wire. Each side is left trusting the other — and the paperwork rarely lines up.

The delay

Months to turn assets into cash

Selling a home, transferring a brokerage account, moving crypto — every channel is separate, manual, and easy to stall. Closure waits on logistics.

The suspicion

"Where did the money go?"

Without a shared record, one late disclosure or missing account turns a settlement into a fight — and back into court.

How Equita works

One flow, from court order to final dollar.

Nothing moves without agreement. Each asset advances through the same four steps — and each step is signed and recorded.

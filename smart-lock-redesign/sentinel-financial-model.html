Why the headline is the point. A storage REIT is valued on NOI at a cap rate, so at a 5.5% cap every $1/yr of recurring NOI adds ≈ $18 of asset value — immediately, at the multiple, not over a payback period. That's why a program with a multi-year cash payback still creates value many times its capex the moment the NOI is stabilized and underwritten. Owning the stack (vs. renting Nokē / DaVinci / Storage Defender) is what lets ~100% of the fee fall to NOI instead of leaking to a vendor.

Assumptions, not promises. Defaults are illustrative placeholders — a ~$6/mo fee tracks Nokē's published tech fee; occupancy, adoption, capex, and opex must be replaced with NCSS actuals and quoted BOM/install. NPV includes a terminal value equal to the capitalized NOI discounted from the horizon year; it excludes financing, taxes, deployment ramp timing, and churn beyond the adoption haircut. Not investment advice — pressure-test with your CFO before underwriting.
